# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Standards

Behavior we want:

- Being considerate and patient with other people
- Respecting differing viewpoints, ideas, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Behavior that is not acceptable here:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information -- identifiers, session content,
  or the text of tasks they ran -- without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer. All complaints
are reviewed and investigated at the maintainer's discretion, and are treated as
confidential.
